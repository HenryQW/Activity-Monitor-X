Calendar shows the history as a grid. Each cell is a block of time with its mean and the busiest app. Use it to find the days and hours when your Mac was busy, then open them in Apps.

## Periods

The bar at the top has **Day**, **Week** and **Month**, the time zone, **Show**, and ‹ **Today** ›.

- **Month** shows one cell for each day. **Week** and **Day** show blocks of hours.
- Double-click a cell to zoom in: Month to Week, Week to Day, Day to Hour.
- To zoom out, press Escape, click the Back button in the toolbar, or choose View > Zoom Out (⌘[). View > Zoom In is ⌘].
- The title in the toolbar says where you are, for example "Week of 1 Nov".

## Cells

- Each cell shows its mean, one line for each part of the cell, and the icon of its busiest app.
- **Show** chooses what the cells count: Busiest App, All Apps, or one of your groups.
- A time with no data, or a time that is still to come, is blank. Its preview says "No data".
- CPU cells use the colours of the System CPU rule: yellow above the warning level and red above the critical level. Memory cells use the memory pressure level. The other metrics have no status colour.
- Hover a cell for a preview with the top apps.

In Day and Hour, the unit is the bar: a bar for each 5 minutes in Day, and for each minute in Hour. Left and Right move the selected bar. Shift with Left or Right extends the selection. Double-click a bar in Day to open that hour.

## Select and compare

- Click a cell to see it in the inspector: its mean, its peak and its top apps.
- Drag across cells to select a span. The inspector compares the span with the span before it.
- Right-click a cell for Show Week, Show Day or Show Hour, and **Show in Apps**, which opens that time on the [Apps](/apps) screen.

## Time zones

The pop-up beside Day, Week and Month shows the time zone ("SGT"). Choose another zone to see every screen in that zone: dates, day boundaries and chart axes. Week and Day can also add hour labels for more zones.

## The footer

The footer shows the cells of the period as a small heat grid, then the mean, the busiest time, the top app with its share, and the alert counts.
